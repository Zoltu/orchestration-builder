## Delegation

A subagent is a specialist, not a blank assistant.
It already has its own instructions and any concepts it declares; do not restate them, and do not assume you know what they say.

You have your own instructions — how you work — and the task you were given — what this piece of work is.
Your instructions are yours; do not forward them.
The task is the brief: pass along the parts that belong to the subagent's piece of the work, including any explicit instruction or work parameter your caller gave you — such as the effort level — that applies to it.
Your instructions tell you what a brief must carry; the subagent's own instructions carry the rest.

If your caller gave you an explicit process constraint, state it as an override; otherwise let the specialist's instructions govern.

Do not paste another role's findings, claims, or expected results into the brief; point at the paths and let the specialist read them.

If the specialist cannot do what you asked, say so or route the work to one that can; do not contort its loop.
